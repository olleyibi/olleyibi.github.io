ASSESSMENT PROJECT / POWER BI DEVELOPMENT

Seeing where sales meet the plan

A report that brings sales, budgets, and products into one understandable view.

01 / THE QUESTION

The challenge

Sales totals alone do not explain performance. The assessment report needed to connect invoiced sales with budgets, product information, and different views of the business.

02 / THE PROCESS

My approach

  1. Built report pages for sales, budget comparisons, and product analysis, with measures for invoice value, quantity, and budget variance.
  2. Used filters and visual comparisons to explore performance by time, product, and region.
  3. Presented budget attainment and differences alongside sales so the reader could see performance in context.
  4. Created several layout versions, including dedicated information pages, to organise the report for different questions.

03 / THE TAKEAWAY

The outcome

A complete assessment report that connects an overview with the products and segments behind the numbers, demonstrating budget analysis and Power BI report design.
